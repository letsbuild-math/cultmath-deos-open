# C7 · Judgment You Can Be Wrong About

**Module:** Core · **Version:** 1.0

> **No verdict without a range, and no judgment without the thing that would prove it wrong.**

## When to use

- Reviewing someone's work, including your own.
- Giving a forecast, a rating or a recommendation.
- Any time you say "good," "weak," "will work" or "won't work."

## The standard

1. **State the standard before the work.** The person being judged should know the bar in advance.
2. **Raise concerns within one cycle.** A problem you saw last week and mention next quarter is an ambush.
3. **Every judgment carries its falsifier.** "This headline will underperform. I'm wrong if it beats the control by more than 10%."
4. **Attributed, specific and testable, or unsaid.** Who thinks it, about what exactly, and how we would know.
5. **Announce changed standards.** If the bar moves, say so before you judge against the new one.
6. **The receiving test.** Before you deliver a judgment, read it as the person receiving it. Can they act on it? Can they check it?
7. **No verdict without a range.** "Likely to hit 40–60 sign-ups," not "will do well."

**Levels of judgment.** Promote someone's level of trust before you promote their title. Never lower someone's trust level without telling them.

## How to run it

1. Write the standard you are judging against.
2. Write the judgment in one sentence, with a range if it is a forecast.
3. Write the falsifier.
4. Attribute it: "My read is…"
5. Read it as the receiver. Rewrite until they could act on it.

## Done when

- The judgment names its standard, its range and its falsifier.
- The receiver could check it without asking you.

## Common failures

- **"It just doesn't feel right."** Not testable. Find what specifically is off, or hold the comment.
- **A standard revealed only in the critique.**
- **Point forecasts with no range.** You will be wrong and nobody will know by how much.

## Template

```markdown
# Judgment: [subject]

- **Standard (stated [date]):**
- **Judgment:** [one sentence, with range]
- **Falsifier:** I'm wrong if [ ]
- **Whose read:**
- **Receiver could act on it?** [yes / rewrite]
```
