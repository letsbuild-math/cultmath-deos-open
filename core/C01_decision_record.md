# C1 · Decision Record

**Module:** Core · **Version:** 1.0 · **From DEOS:** cm_22

> **A decision you did not write down cannot be reviewed, defended or learned from.**

## When to use

- Any decision that changes what you build, sell, spend or promise.
- Always for: pricing, a deviation from a standard, a cost that grows with volume, anything hard to reverse, and anything decided under time pressure.
- Not for: choices you will remake tomorrow at no cost.

## The standard

1. **One record per decision.** Not per meeting, not per project.
2. **Every record carries a status.** One of five:
   - **CONFIRMED**: decided and already in effect.
   - **DECIDED**: chosen, not yet in effect.
   - **PENDING**: needs a decision; the record names who decides and by when.
   - **DEFERRED**: consciously put off; the record names the date or event that reopens it.
   - **REVISED**: replaced by a later record, which it links to. Never deleted.
3. **The evidence is named, not summarized.** Link the brief, the data, the conversation. "We looked at it" is not evidence.
4. **The tradeoff is written out.** What you gave up by choosing this. A decision with no cost listed has not been thought through.
5. **Every record names its trigger for reconsideration.** The specific fact, date or number that would reopen it.
6. **Records are never edited after the fact to look smarter.** A changed mind is a new record with status REVISED on the old one.

## How to run it

1. Write the decision as one sentence in the active voice: "We will price the pilot at a fixed fee."
2. Pick the status.
3. List the evidence with links.
4. Write the tradeoff in one or two sentences.
5. Write the trigger for reconsideration as something you could check.
6. File it in `decisions/` with the date in the file name.
7. If it replaces an older record, mark the old one REVISED and link both ways.

## Done when

- Someone who was not in the room can read it and say what was decided, why, what it cost, and what would change it.
- The trigger is concrete enough that you would notice it happening.

## Common failures

- **The record restates the discussion.** Cut it to the decision, the evidence and the tradeoff.
- **No tradeoff.** Every real decision gives something up. Name it.
- **The trigger is "if things change."** Name the number or event.
- **Silent revision.** The old record is overwritten. History is how you learn which of your calls were right.

## Template

```markdown
# Decision: [one sentence]

- **Status:** CONFIRMED | DECIDED | PENDING | DEFERRED | REVISED
- **Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
- **Decided by:** [name or role]
- **Evidence:** [links]
- **Tradeoff acknowledged:** [what this gives up]
- **Trigger for reconsideration:** [the fact, date or number that reopens it]
- **Replaces / replaced by:** [link, if any]
```
