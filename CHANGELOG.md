# Changelog

## 1.3 · November 17, 2026

- **New: C14 The Humane Check** (Core). Four questions to run before a decision that lands on other people: reason, empathy, autonomy, legacy. Distilled from DEOS's Humanist Core. Core is now 14 standards; DEOS Open is 55.
- README, START-HERE, core/README, `llms.txt` and `standards.json` updated.

## 1.2 · November 16, 2026

- **For agents:** `llms.txt` and `standards.json`, built from the standard files by `tools/build-index.mjs`.
- **Schemas:** JSON Schemas for the Decision Record (C1), Brief (C3), Gate (C5) and Six-Lens Board (C13), with a passing and a failing example of each.
- **Checker:** `tools/check.mjs`, no dependencies, plus `tools/check.test.mjs`, which confirms every failing example fails.
- C1, C3, C5 and C13 link to their schema.
- The CULT+MATH connector serves the standards (`list_standards`, `get_standard`).

## 1.1 · November 16, 2026

- **CONTRIBUTING.md:** three ways to help, house style, how changes are decided, and contribution licensing (same as the repo).
- **Issue forms:** Report a mistake, Request a standard, I used it.
- **Discussions** turned on for questions and show-and-tell.
- **Feedback without GitHub:** a form at cultmath.com/deos.
- README and START-HERE link to the cultmath.com/deos setup guide.

## 1.0 · November 16, 2026

First release. 54 standards in five parts:

- **Core (13):** C1–C12 from DEOS, plus **C13 The Six-Lens Board**.
- **Research (10):** includes **R3 Category Entry Point Read**, new in DEOS Open, built on the Ehrenberg-Bass Institute's published method.
- **Marketing & Brand (13):** includes **M13 The Sell-In Story**, new in DEOS Open, for products sold through distributors to operators.
- **Product & Innovation (8)**
- **Projects & People (10)**

Also: README, START-HERE, LICENSE (CC BY 4.0 for text, MIT for templates), and a starter workspace.
