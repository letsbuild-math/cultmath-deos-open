# W7 · Advisor Sessions

**Module:** Projects & People · **Version:** 1.0 · **From DEOS:** cm_09

> **Advice is valuable only if it is captured, classified and routed to a decision.**

## When to use

- After a session with a mentor, advisor, senior colleague, manager or board member who gave you direction.

## The standard

1. **Keep a short profile of each advisor:** what they know deeply, where their advice has been right before, and their known biases.
2. **Session record:** date, topic, what they said (in their words where it matters), and what they asked you.
3. **Classify each input:**
   - **Direction:** a specific recommendation.
   - **Challenge:** a question or doubt about your current plan.
   - **Information:** a fact, contact or resource.
   - **Perspective:** how they see the situation, without a recommendation.
4. **Route each input to one of:**
   - **Act:** it becomes a task or a decision record (C1).
   - **Test:** it goes to the question register (C8) with how you will check it.
   - **Hold:** noted, not acted on now, with why.
   - **Decline:** you disagree; write why. Advice you decline is still recorded.
5. **When two advisors disagree,** do not average them. Run conflict triage (W6, stage 0).
6. **Close the loop:** tell them what you did with their advice.

## How to run it

1. Write the session record the same day.
2. Classify and route every input.
3. Update the advisor's profile if this changes how you weigh them.
4. Follow up with them when the routed items resolve.

## Done when

- Every input is classified and routed.
- The advisor has heard what you did.

## Common failures

- **Treating every piece of advice as direction.**
- **Quietly ignoring advice** instead of recording the decline.
- **Splitting the difference** between two advisors.

## Template

```markdown
## Advisor session · [name] · [date]
| Input | Type (Direction/Challenge/Information/Perspective) | Route (Act/Test/Hold/Decline) | Why / next step |
|---|---|---|---|
**Loop closed on:**
```
