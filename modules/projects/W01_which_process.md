# W1 · Which Process

**Module:** Projects & People · **Version:** 1.0 · **From DEOS:** cm_08

> **Don't spend energy deciding which standard to use. Answer the question that fits your moment and run what it points to.**

## When to use

- Any time you are not sure which DEOS Open standard applies.

## The standard

Answer these in order. The first yes tells you what to run.

| # | Question | Run |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Am I about to make a decision that changes what we build, sell, spend or promise? | C1 Decision Record (and C2 if it is hard to reverse) |
| 2 | Am I about to research, test or analyze something? | C3 Brief Written First, then the Research module |
| 3 | Did I just show something to a customer, user or partner? | R8 Customer Feedback Review, or P2 for a product session |
| 4 | Did an advisor or senior colleague just give me direction? | W7 Advisor Sessions |
| 5 | Are two strong inputs pointing opposite ways and I'm stuck? | W6 Raw Thinking to Strategy, starting with conflict triage |
| 6 | Do I have a meeting coming up? | W3 Meeting Brief; W4 if three or more parties must decide |
| 7 | Did a long session just end? | W5 Capture the Session |
| 8 | Did something go wrong? | C9 Failure Register |
| 9 | Am I writing something someone will act on? | C11 Write for One Reader; C13 if it is a recommendation |
| 10 | Is it the end of the day, Friday, or month-end? | C12 The Rhythm |
| 11 | Am I new to a job, client or market? | R1 Map the Ground First, then R2 Category Read |
| 12 | Am I asking an AI to do work for me? | W8 Working With AI |

**Rules:**

- If two apply, run the earlier one first.
- If none apply, you probably do not need a standard. Do the work.

## Done when

- You picked a standard in under a minute.

## Common failures

- **Running every standard on everything.** Most work needs none.
