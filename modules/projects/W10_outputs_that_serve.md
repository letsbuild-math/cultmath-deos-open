# W10 · Outputs That Serve

**Module:** Projects & People · **Version:** 1.0

> **Marketing that works by hiding something from the buyer is extraction. Every output says who bears its cost.**

## When to use

- Before any campaign, segment, offer, pricing page, message sequence or AI-generated output goes live.

## The standard

1. **Answer three questions for every output:**
   1. **Who bears the cost if this works?** If the answer is the buyer, in a way they would object to if they could see it, change it.
   2. **Does every message cite its evidence, or is it marked as opinion?**
   3. **What could this be misused for?** Name it, and the guard against it.
2. **Four refusals.** Do not build:
   1. **Comparisons that hide a cost or quantity** the buyer should see.
   2. **Segments defined by reduced ability to resist:** addiction, debt distress, grief, cognitive decline, children.
   3. **Manufactured urgency or scarcity.** Real deadlines and real limits are facts. Invented ones are lies with a timer.
   4. **Dependency loops** designed to drive repeat purchase against the buyer's interest.
3. **This does not ban persuasion.** It bans persuasion that only works if the buyer cannot see how it works (W9's test applies).
4. **If you are asked to build a refused output,** say so plainly, propose the honest version, and record the decision (C1).

## How to run it

1. Answer the three questions in writing.
2. Check against the four refusals.
3. Fix, or decline and record.

## Done when

- The three answers are written and no refusal applies.

## Common failures

- **"Only 3 left!"** when there are three hundred.
- **Targeting people at their weakest moment** because it converts.
- **Hiding the per-unit price** in a bundle.

## Template

```markdown

2. Evidence or marked as opinion:
3. Possible misuse · guard:
**Refusal check:** hidden cost [ ] · vulnerable segment [ ] · fake urgency [ ] · dependency loop [ ]
```
