# M6 · Identity Ladder

**Module:** Marketing & Brand · **Version:** 1.0 · **From DEOS:** cm_114

> **Build a brand from purpose down to voice, in order, starting from what it actually shipped.**

## When to use

- A new brand, a rebrand, a sub-brand, or a brand whose team cannot agree on what it is.

## The standard

1. **Seven rungs, built in order:**
   1. **Purpose:** why it exists beyond making money.
   2. **Vision:** what is different in the world if it succeeds.
   3. **Values:** each with a definition (what it means here), a boundary (what it forbids) and a test (how you would know it was broken). A value without a boundary is decoration.
   4. **Audience:** who it is for and who it is not for (M1).
   5. **Positioning and proposition** (M4).
   6. **Name and tagline.**
   7. **Voice:** how it sounds, with do and don't examples.
2. **Five stages:**
   1. **Audit what shipped.** Read only what the brand actually put out (site, packs, ads, posts), not the brief. That is what the brand currently is.
   2. **Name the absences.** Which rungs are missing or contradicted by what shipped?
   3. **Build the ladder** rung by rung. Each rung must follow from the one above.
   4. **Enforce what can be enforced:** checklists, templates, banned-word lists, review gates.
   5. **Roll it out through a register:** every surface listed, updated, and checked off.
3. **A rung cannot contradict the rung above it.** If voice fights values, fix the ladder.

## How to run it

1. Collect every shipped surface. Audit them only.
2. List what is missing.
3. Draft the seven rungs in order.
4. Write the enforcement checks.
5. Build the rollout register and work through it.

## Done when

- Seven rungs, each consistent with the one above.
- Every value has a boundary and a test.
- Every surface in the register is updated.

## Common failures

- **Starting at the tagline.**
- **Auditing the brand book** instead of what shipped.
- **Values with no boundary.**
