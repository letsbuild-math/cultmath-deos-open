# M12 · Signal → Score → Story

**Module:** Marketing & Brand · **Version:** 1.0 · **From DEOS:** cm_61, cm_65, cm_68

> **Decide what to publish by rule, send it where the buyer decides, pay only to push what already worked, and measure what AI engines say about you.**

## When to use

- Running a content program: deciding what to write, where to post it, whether to boost it, and how to measure it.

## The standard

### 1. Signal: what to write

Watch three families of signal each week: moments (news, launches, events), market moves (competitor and category changes), and audience language (R7).

**Two gates, pass both or drop it:**

- **Right to speak:** do you have a true, distinct angle on this that fits your position?
- **Proof:** can every factual claim be sourced?

**Three scores, 0–3 each:**

| Score | Question |
|---|---|
| Relevance | How directly does it connect to what you stand for? |
| Resonance | How much does the audience already care? |
| Leverage | How many outputs can one piece of thinking feed? |

**Decay sets the timing, not the worth:** hours/days, weeks, or evergreen.

| Score | Action |
|---|---|
| 7–9, short decay | Act now |
| 7–9, longer decay | Plan it |
| 4–6 | Watch. If it recurs 3+ times, promote it. |
| 0–3, or fails a gate | Drop |

### 2. Distribution: where it goes

- **Channel fit:** post where the buyer decides, not where reach is biggest.
- **Personal beats corporate** on most social platforms.
- **Judge each piece by one metric** tied to business, such as qualified conversations, not likes.
- **Pay only to push a proven post further,** with a cap. Never pay to manufacture reach.

### 3. Measurement: AI visibility

- Track two things separately: **AI traffic** (visits from AI engines, via an analytics channel group matching AI referrers) and **AI visibility** (how often engines mention or cite you, clicked or not).
- Put the AI channel above Referral in your analytics channel order so visits are not misfiled.
- Check server logs for AI crawlers fetching your pages.
- State what analytics will never show: answers that mention you with no click.

## How to run it

1. Weekly, 30–45 minutes: scan, gate, score, act/plan/watch/drop.
2. For each piece: pick the channel and the one metric.
3. After a week: grade it. Boost only a proven winner, with a cap.
4. Monthly: read AI traffic and AI visibility.

## Done when

- Each published piece passed both gates and has a score.
- Each boost was on a proven piece, with a cap.

## Common failures

- **Writing about what everyone is discussing** without a right to speak.
- **Boosting a post that did not work organically.**
- **Counting only AI clicks** and missing mentions.
