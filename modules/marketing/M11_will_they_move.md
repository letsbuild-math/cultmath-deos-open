# M11 · Will They Move?

**Module:** Marketing & Brand · **Version:** 1.0

> **Find out whether a person is ready to change before you try to change them.**

## When to use

- Before a pitch, proposal, internal change request or campaign aimed at a specific buyer type.

## The standard

1. **Five terms, asked in order:**

   | # | Term | The question | Satisfied by | If skipped |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | **Who** | Who specifically, by situation, not sector | A situation you would recognize in a room | You market to a category, not a person |
   | 2 | **When** | What state are they in right now that makes this live? | A condition with a clock on it, or a visible ceiling | "Great, not now" |
   | 3 | **Why** | What is the pain, and what causes it? | The cause, in their words | You sell against a symptom they have learned to live with |
   | 4 | **What holds** | What specifically do they lose by moving? | One named thing they would grieve | Deals that die for reasons nobody says |
   | 5 | **What releases** | What makes the loss survivable? | Something they can check, not a claim you make | A feature looking for a buyer |

2. **The stated hold is rarely the real one.** Three layers:
   - **Stated:** practical and defensible. "The money."
   - **Real:** identity. What the current way proves about them.
   - **Floor:** worth. What they fear is true if things can work without them.
3. **The kill test.** If you cannot fill What holds and What releases, do not build the pitch. They are not movable yet, or you do not understand them yet.

## How to run it

1. Fill the five terms from conversations and evidence (R7).
2. For What holds, go past the stated reason.
3. Run the kill test.
4. If it passes, build the piece with M10, aimed at What releases.

## Done when

- All five terms filled, with What holds named past the stated layer.

## Common failures

- **Taking "budget" at face value.**
- **Answering What releases with your own claims** instead of something they can check.
