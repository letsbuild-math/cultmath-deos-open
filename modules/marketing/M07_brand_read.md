# M7 · Brand Read

**Module:** Marketing & Brand · **Version:** 1.0

> **Read what a brand is actually known for, who decides, and what moves them, before you write a word of content.**

## When to use

- Before building a content program, a thought-leadership plan or a personal brand.
- When you need a position and content pillars backed by evidence.

## The standard

1. **Five layers, in order:**

   | Layer | Question | Typical sources |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Truth** | What are they actually known for? | Review themes, own statements, case studies, credentials, firsts |
   | **Market** | Who decides whether they get the work? | Category buyers, account lists, referral sources |
   | **Competition** | Who else could the buyer pick? | Share of search, ad activity, competitor positioning |
   | **Drivers** | What moves each buyer? | Buyer research, win/loss, published studies |
   | **Position → pillars → posts** | One line, three proof points, a never-say list; pillars mapped to buyer and driver; each post tagged | Built from the four layers above |

2. **Mark every figure** reported, counted or judgment.
3. **State the scope first:** what was read and what was not.
4. **Write themes before counting.** Then count against them, and state the sample (n of N).
5. **Proof points must be public** and the brand's to use.
6. **Every pillar names its buyer and the driver it serves.** A pillar serving no named driver is cut.
7. **The owner approves** the position and pillars before the first post. Record the date.
8. **The read expires** when the role, location or offer changes, or after 12 months.
9. **Results feed back by pillar** at day 30 and day 90. Re-weight on evidence, not taste.

## How to run it

1. Write the scope.
2. Fill Truth, Market, Competition and Drivers.
3. Draft the position line, three proof points and the never-say list.
4. Map pillars to buyer × driver.
5. Get approval. Launch. Review at 30 and 90 days.

## Done when

- Each pillar names a buyer and a driver.
- Approval is dated.
- A 30-day review is scheduled.

## Common failures

- **Pillars chosen by what is fun to write.**
- **Private testimonials used as public proof.**
- **No review,** so the mix never changes.

## Template

```markdown
**Scope:** read · not read
| Layer | Finding | Mark (reported/counted/judgment) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
**Position:** · **Proof points:** 1. 2. 3. · **Never say:**
| Pillar | Buyer | Driver | Share of posts |
|---|---|---|---|
**Approved:** [date] · **Review:** day 30 [date], day 90 [date]
```
