# R5 · Evidence That Proves

**Module:** Research · **Version:** 1.0 · **From DEOS:** cm_96

> **Build the artifact so it shows the reader something they could not have known, from sources they can check.**

## When to use

- Any research deck, competitive read, market brief or point-of-view piece that names other companies.

## The standard

1. **Run the teach-me-something-new filter on every finding:**

   | Type | Means | Do |
   |---|---|---|
   | **New** | This reader could not know this. | Lead with it. This is the artifact. |
   | **Known** | This reader lived it, built it or approved it. | Cut it, or use it only as evidence. |
   | **Method** | Needed to defend it, not for insight. | Move to the notes. |

2. **Verify before you design.** No chart is built on a number that has not been checked against its source.
3. **Cut, do not label.** If a point needs "interesting" in front of it, it is not.
4. **Show where each fact came from.** Mark primary, secondary and estimated sources visibly, the same way every time.
5. **Hedges go in one place,** next to the claim they qualify, not spread across every sentence.
6. **Publish your corrections** as visibly as the original.
7. **About named third parties:**
   - No scores, rankings, quadrants or axis placements. Putting companies on axes is a judgment dressed as a diagram.
   - No health or performance claims beyond what they or a regulator published.
   - Observations may state a pattern; they may not tell a named company what to do.
   - Primary sources only. Aggregators are leads. Tool output is a draft.
   - Everything dated, with how long it stays true.
   - Neutral to generous toward every named party.
   - Have counsel review the template once before the first external use.

## How to run it

1. List every finding. Mark New, Known or Method.
2. Verify each New finding against a primary source.
3. Build the artifact around the New findings only.
4. Mark sources visibly.
5. Check every third-party mention against the rules in 7.

## Done when

- Every finding left in the body is New to this reader and traced to a primary source.
- No named company is scored or placed on axes.

## Common failures

- **Telling the reader their own business.**
- **The 2x2 with competitors' logos on it.**
- **A secondary source cited as if it were primary.**

## Template

```markdown
| Finding | New / Known / Method | Primary source | Date | Goes stale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
```
