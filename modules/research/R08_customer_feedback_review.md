# R8 · Customer Feedback Review

**Module:** Research · **Version:** 1.0 · **From DEOS:** cm_07

> **Feedback that is not recorded, sorted and answered within a week is lost.**

## When to use

- After any session where a product, campaign, prototype or idea was shown to a customer, prospect, partner or advisor.

## The standard

Three parts, in order, within 48 hours of the session.

1. **Session record.** Who, when, what was shown, what they did, what they said. Facts only. Quote their words. Note what they did without being asked.
2. **Signal extraction.** Sort each observation:
   - **Confirms:** supports a current belief.
   - **Challenges:** contradicts a current belief.
   - **New:** something you had not considered.
   - **Noise:** one person's preference with no sign it generalizes.

   Mark each as a one-off or a repeat (seen in earlier sessions). A repeat outranks a strong one-off.
3. **Response plan.** For each signal you will act on: the change, the owner, the date. For each you will not: why not. Then tell the person what you did with their feedback.

**Rules:**

- Behavior outranks opinion. What they did beats what they said they would do.
- Do not defend the product during the session. Ask "what makes you say that?"
- Praise is the weakest signal. Look for what they tried to do and could not.

## How to run it

1. Write the session record the same day.
2. Within 48 hours, sort the signals and check them against past sessions.
3. Write the response plan.
4. Close the loop with the person within a week.

## Done when

- Every signal is sorted.
- Every acted-on signal has an owner and a date.
- The person has heard back.

## Common failures

- **Recording interpretations instead of observations.**
- **Acting on the loudest voice.**
- **Never closing the loop,** so people stop giving feedback.

## Template

```markdown
## Session · [date] · [who]
- Shown:
- Did (unprompted):
- Said (verbatim):

## Signals
| Observation | Confirms / Challenges / New / Noise | One-off / Repeat |
|---|---|---|

## Response
| Signal | Action or reason not | Owner | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
**Loop closed with person on:**
```
